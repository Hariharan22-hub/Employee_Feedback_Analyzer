# Organizational Attendance and Working Hours Policy

1. Core Working Hours
- Standard operational working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday (40 hours per week).
- Flexible working window: Employees may adjust start times between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM upon agreement with their department manager, ensuring presence during core collaboration hours (10:00 AM - 3:00 PM).

2. Time Tracking and Check-In
- All non-exempt employees must record their daily working hours via the digital HR attendance system.
- Punctuality is essential for cross-functional synchronization, scheduled client engagements, and stand-ups.

3. Tardiness and Unscheduled Absence
- Notice of unavoidable delays or unscheduled absence must be communicated to the team lead or reporting manager at least 30 minutes before the scheduled shift start.
- Three or more unexcused tardiness instances in a single month will trigger a review with HR and may lead to performance improvement discussions.

4. Overtime & Compensation Time
- Non-exempt staff required to work beyond 40 hours per week are eligible for 1.5x overtime compensation, subject to advance supervisory approval.
- Exempt salaried professionals may take compensatory off-hours during low-intensity project periods with manager approval.

5. Breaks & Meal Periods
- Employees are entitled to a mandatory 60-minute meal break for shifts over 6 hours, plus two 15-minute wellness breaks.

# Remote Work and Hybrid Work-From-Home (WFH) Policy

1. Hybrid Model Structure
- The organization operates on an agile hybrid model: employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, with a minimum of 2 days on-site collaboration at the company office.
- Core on-site team days are set by individual department heads (commonly Tuesdays and Thursdays for all-hands and planning sessions).

2. Fully Remote Eligibility
- Full remote status is assessed on a role-by-role basis for specialized roles or geographically dispersed staff. Requires approval by the Department VP and Chief People Officer.
- Employees must maintain consistent high performance and reliable metrics to remain eligible for remote privileges.

3. Home Office Equipment and Security Stipend
- All eligible remote employees receive standard IT corporate hardware: encrypted laptop, external monitor, and enterprise peripherals.
- A one-time home office ergonomic setup stipend of $500 is provided to new full-time hires.
- A monthly internet connectivity allowance of $50 is reimbursed through standard expense claims.

4. Cybersecurity & Data Protection Requirements
- All remote work must be conducted exclusively via the secure corporate VPN.
- Company devices must never be shared with family members or unauthorized third parties.
- Public Wi-Fi networks must only be utilized with active corporate VPN encryption and Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

5. Availability and Communication
- Remote employees must maintain active status indicators on enterprise collaboration tools (Slack/Teams) during agreed core business hours.
- Video presence is encouraged during key team ceremonies and quarterly milestone reviews.

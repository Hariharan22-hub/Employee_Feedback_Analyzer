# Workplace Ethics and Code of Conduct Policy

1. Core Values and Ethical Standard
Every employee is expected to uphold integrity, mutual respect, professional excellence, and ethical accountability across all workplace interactions, communications, and client representations.

2. Equal Employment and Anti-Discrimination
- The company maintains a zero-tolerance policy against unlawful discrimination or harassment on the grounds of race, gender, religion, age, sexual orientation, disability, nationality, or any legally protected status.
- Harassment includes unwelcomed sexual advances, derogatory remarks, intimidation, or hostility. Violators face immediate disciplinary sanctions up to termination.

3. Conflicts of Interest
- Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or familial interests that could potentially impair independent professional judgment.
- Moonlighting or secondary outside employment requires prior written authorization from HR and department management.

4. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property
- Proprietary information, internal analytics, customer data, and source code are strictly confidential company property.
- Unauthorized disclosure or misuse during or after employment will result in legal action and termination.

5. Workplace Safety and Health
- The company is committed to maintaining a safe, healthy, and substance-free work environment.
- Any hazard, injury, or safety violation must be promptly reported to Operations and HR.

6. Notice Period and Separation
- Standard voluntary resignation notice period is 30 calendar days for individual contributors and 60 calendar days for managers and executives.
- Accelerated release or payment-in-lieu of notice is subject to mutual agreement and department head consent.

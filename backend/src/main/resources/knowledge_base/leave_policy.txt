# Organizational Leave Policy

1. Overview
The company provides comprehensive leave benefits to support employee well-being, work-life integration, personal commitments, and rest.

2. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Annual Vacation
- Full-time permanent employees are allocated 24 days of paid annual vacation per calendar year, accrued monthly at 2 days per full month of service.
- Annual leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days must be submitted through the HR Portal at least two weeks in advance for manager approval.
- Up to 8 unused annual leave days can be carried over into the next calendar year, expiring on June 30 of the following year.

3. Sick Leave & Medical Care
- Employees receive 12 paid sick days per year, credited on January 1st.
- For sick leave of 3 or more consecutive days, a licensed medical practitioner's certificate is required upon return to work.
- In cases of prolonged illness or hospitalization, employees may apply for Medical Leave of Absence under corporate health insurance benefits.

4. Parental & Family Leave
- Primary caregivers (maternity) are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave.
- Secondary caregivers (paternity) are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid paternity leave.
- Adoption and surrogacy leave provide 10 weeks of paid leave for primary caregivers.
- Bereavement leave provides up to 5 consecutive paid days for immediate family members (parents, spouse, children, siblings) and 3 days for extended family.

5. Casual & Emergency Leave
- 5 days of casual leave are provided for unforeseen urgent personal obligations.
- Prior notification should be sent to the immediate reporting manager before start of business.

6. Unpaid Sabbatical
- Employees with 3 or more years of continuous tenure may apply for unpaid educational or sabbatical leave for a period ranging from 1 to 6 months with prior executive and HR approval.

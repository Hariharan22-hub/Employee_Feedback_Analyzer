# Employee Grievance Redressal and Resolution Policy

1. Policy Objective
This policy establishes an impartial, transparent, confidential, and timely mechanism for employees to raise grievances concerning unfair treatment, workplace conditions, harassment, or conflicts.

2. Step 1 - Informal Resolution
- Employees are encouraged to resolve minor misunderstandings or interpersonal friction informally with their peer or direct supervisor within 10 business days of the incident.
- HR People Partners are available for informal neutral mediation coaching without formal documentation if preferred.

3. Step 2 - Formal Grievance Submission
- If the matter remains unresolved or involves direct managerial conflict, the employee may submit a written grievance through the secure Employee Feedback Portal or directly to grievance@company.internal.
- The complaint must detail dates, involved parties, factual evidence, and desired resolution.

4. Step 3 - Impartial Investigation
- The HR Ethics Committee assigns an independent investigator within 3 business days of receiving the grievance.
- All involved parties are interviewed with strict confidentiality protections.
- The investigator prepares an objective findings report within 14 business days.

5. Step 4 - Determination and Action
- The Grievance Committee delivers written recommendations and remedial actions to the concerned parties within 5 business days of investigation conclusion.
- Corrective actions may range from structural reallocation, mandatory training, formal reprimand, to employment termination.

6. Anti-Retaliation Guarantee
- Retaliation against any employee who reports a grievance in good faith or participates in an investigation is strictly prohibited under zero-tolerance rules, punishable by immediate termination.
